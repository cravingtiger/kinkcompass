polysexuell | Polysexual | | Attraction to several, but not all, genders.
sapiosexuell | Sapiosexual | | Intelligence and ways of thinking as the central trigger of attraction.
abrosexuell | Abrosexual | | Your own orientation shifts over time.
lithsexuell-akiosexuell | Lithsexual / akiosexual | | Attraction is present, but having it returned is not wanted.

bellussexuell | Bellusexual | | Interest in romantic and sensual aspects without wanting a relationship.
